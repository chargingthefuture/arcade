    What makes these games different

    These are not marketing lines. They are the rules every game in this arcade has to follow.

    No ads, ever

    You will never see an advertisement, a paywall, or a nag screen. There is nothing to buy and nothing to upsell.

    No tracking

    No accounts, no analytics, no cookies following you around. The games do not phone home, and this page loads nothing from outside services.

    Offline-first

    Once a game is on your device it keeps working with the internet turned off. It cannot be silently changed by a forced update while you sleep.

    Open source

    Every game’s full source code is public. Anyone can read exactly what it does — there is no hidden behavior to trust on faith.

    No harassment

    These are single-player, self-contained games. There are no chat rooms, no strangers, and no channel for anyone to reach you through a game.

    Yours to keep

    Download a game and it is yours. No login can be revoked, no server can be shut off to take it away from you.

    Why this arcade exists

    Farah’s Arcade is a companion to the TI Skills Economy — a peer-to-peer marketplace where survivors of human trafficking help each other rebuild their lives and reach real independence, from housing to transportation, without manipulation or surveillance.

    The mainstream app stores are not always safe ground. A game downloaded today can be replaced tomorrow by a forced update that adds harassment, invades privacy, and exists to agitate the person playing it. That is the opposite of rest.

    This arcade is a small answer to that. It gathers free, open-source, offline-first games in one calm place, so that someone who needs a quiet moment can have one — with no ads, no tracking, and no way for a game to be turned into a tool against them.
